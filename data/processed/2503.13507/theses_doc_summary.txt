The NeurIPS 2023 LLM Efficiency Challenge, which tasked participants with fine-tuning open-source models under strict resource constraints, revealed that top-performing models suffered from significant overfitting on open benchmark datasets, leading to poor generalization on unseen tasks. The winning submissions achieved success primarily through meticulous data curation and the use of standard open-source libraries rather than custom architectural changes. Additionally, the competition exposed widespread reproducibility issues, with over half of the submitted Dockerfiles failing to build due to unpinned dependencies. In response, the organizers released all competition artifacts and evaluation infrastructure to promote reproducibility, while concluding that the machine learning community must adopt more robust, multi-metric, and real-world evaluation standards.
